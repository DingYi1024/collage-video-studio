# Responsive direction

Landscape, portrait, and square are separate editorial decisions, not crops.

For each aspect:

- keep the claim and semantic contracts unchanged;
- relocate title, subject, evidence, and captions through node overrides;
- preserve reading order;
- resize background papers and framing devices to the new canvas;
- re-route annotations and chart labels;
- validate text fit and safe zones;
- render and review the same proof moment in every frame.

Use `director_plans` in the recursive composition manifest. A project may deliver one aspect,
but a reusable template should prove all three. The `editorial-proof-demo` compiles and renders
all variants from one source tree.
